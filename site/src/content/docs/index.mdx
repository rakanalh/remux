Remux keeps your terminal sessions in a background server, so they survive a closed terminal or a dropped connection. It pairs tmux-style persistence with bordered, named panes, a prefix key whose options pop up as you type, and remote machines that sit in the same session tree as your local ones.

## Highlights

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Sessions that stay running" icon="rocket">
		Detach and reattach at will. Sessions are saved to disk and come back when the server restarts.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Remotes over SSH" icon="server">
		Declare a server once and its sessions appear next to your local ones, one keypress away.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Views" icon="window">
		Put panes from different sessions and machines on one screen, each a live, typeable alias.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Seven layouts" icon="random">
		BSP, Master, Monocle, Grid, Columns and Rows arrange panes for you. Custom keeps your own splits.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Discoverable keys" icon="puzzle">
		Press `Ctrl-a` and a which-key popup shows what comes next. Every binding is configurable.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Sidebars and agents" icon="list-format">
		Dock a session tree, a file browser, or a live list of your AI coding agents and what they need.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
